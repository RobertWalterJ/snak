# Credits — Snak

Snak is built from other people's open work. Everything below is used under its own licence. This file is generated by `npm run credits` from the data the app ships.

## Word and sentence data

- **Meanings, genders, sounds and word forms:** English Wiktionary, through [kaikki.org](https://kaikki.org) (CC BY-SA 4.0). A few meanings for the commonest function words were checked by hand because Wiktionary lists a rare meaning first.
- **Example sentences and their English translations:** [Tatoeba](https://tatoeba.org) and its contributors (CC BY 2.0 FR).
- **Word frequency:** [FrequencyWords](https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords) by hermitdave, built from OpenSubtitles (CC BY-SA 4.0).
- **Scheduler:** [ts-fsrs](https://github.com/open-spaced-repetition/ts-fsrs) (MIT), the Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler.
- **Fonts:** Atkinson Hyperlegible (Braille Institute) and Fraunces (Undercase Type), both SIL Open Font Licence 1.1.

## Computer voice

2561 word clips and 2873 sentence clips are made with [Piper](https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl) (MIT/GPL, text-to-speech) using the Danish voice [da_DK-talesyntese-medium](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/da/da_DK/talesyntese/medium), trained on a recording set from the Norwegian National Library (Språkbanken), CC0. These clips are a machine voice, and the app says so.

## Not used on purpose

Tatoeba recordings by one contributor who recorded thousands of sentences in languages they do not speak are left out, because they may not be recordings by a native speaker.
